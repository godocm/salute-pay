What is separate?

Network transaction costs, Solana account rent and any merchant-authorized executor reward are separate from the dashboard subscription. Set the reward to zero and sponsor collection with your own wallet, or offer a reward to independent executors.

The contract remains permissionless. Any merchant can build independent infrastructure around it; dashboard payment-link quotas do not restrict contract access.

Plans are offered for operator activation; self-service billing checkout is not enabled. Availability follows verified network deployments. Paid support requires a staffed support operation and does not promise a response-time SLA. Free and Growth quotas reset each calendar month (UTC).
Salute supplies infrastructure. The protocol forwards funds to the signed merchant.
